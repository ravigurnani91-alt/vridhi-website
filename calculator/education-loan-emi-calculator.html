Frequently asked questions

What is a moratorium period on an education loan?

The moratorium is a period — typically your course duration plus an additional 6–12 months after course completion — during which full EMI repayment is paused. Some lenders require interest-only payments during this period; others allow it to accrue and add to the principal.

Is collateral required for an education loan?

This depends on the loan amount. Many lenders offer collateral-free education loans up to a certain limit (commonly ₹4–7.5 lakh for domestic studies, varies by lender and scheme), with collateral required above that threshold.

Is there a tax benefit on education loan interest?

Yes — under Section 80E of the Income Tax Act, interest paid on an education loan is fully deductible from taxable income for up to 8 years from the start of repayment, with no upper limit on the amount, subject to conditions on the​ Income Tax Department website.
