Before you apply

Five things worth checking on any IPO, regardless of how much buzz it has:

  • Read the Red Herring Prospectus (RHP) — not just news summaries
  • Check the subscription level in each category (QIB, NII, Retail) separately
  • Understand the use-of-proceeds — fresh issue vs. offer-for-sale changes what the IPO is actually funding
  • Compare the price band to listed peers in the same sector, if any exist
  • Grey market premium (GMP) is commonly quoted but is an unregulated, informal indicator — treat it as a rough sentiment gauge, not a guarantee
