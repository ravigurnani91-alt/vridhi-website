Gold Just Hit a Record — Here's What Happened the Last Two Times It Did

Published 26 September 2026 · 3 min read
By Ravi Gurnani
2 cycles
Gold has hit and then held near record highs twice before in the last 15 years — 2011 and 2020

Every time gold sets a new record, the same question follows: is this the top, or the start of a bigger move? History doesn't answer that cleanly, but it does give two real reference points worth knowing — 2011 and 2020.

In both prior cycles, gold's record run was driven by a mix of currency weakness, global uncertainty, and central bank buying — the same ingredients visible in the current run. What differed each time was how long prices held near the peak before consolidating.

For Indian investors, gold's role is usually less about timing the peak and more about the small, steady allocation that holds up when equities wobble — which is why it sits alongside your SIP and FX rates on Vridhi's live tracker, not as a replacement for either.
