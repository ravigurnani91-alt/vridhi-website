Frequently asked questions

Are home loan interest rates fixed or floating?

Most home loans in India today are on floating rates, commonly linked to the RBI's repo rate via each bank's External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR). This means your EMI (or tenure) can change when the repo rate changes. Fixed-rate home loans exist but are less common and usually carry a premium.

What is the maximum tenure for a home loan?

Most lenders offer home loan tenures up to 30 years, though the maximum is often capped so that the loan is fully repaid before the borrower reaches a certain age (commonly 65–70), whichever comes first.

Is there a tax benefit on home loan EMIs?

Yes — under the old tax regime, principal repayment can qualify for a Section 80C deduction (within the overall ₹1.5L limit) and interest paid can qualify for a deduction under Section 24(b), subject to conditions. Check the Income Tax Department site for current rules.
