Frequently asked questions

How is FD interest usually compounded in India?

Most Indian bank FDs compound quarterly by default, though some offer monthly, half-yearly, or annual compounding, and some let you choose a "simple interest" payout option where interest is paid out periodically rather than compounded.

Is FD interest taxable?

Yes — FD interest is fully taxable as per your income tax slab, and banks deduct TDS if your total interest income from that bank exceeds the threshold set under the Income Tax Act. Check the Income Tax Department site for current thresholds.

What is a tax-saving FD?

A 5-year tax-saving FD qualifies for a Section 80C deduction (within the overall ₹1.5L limit) under the old tax regime, but comes with a mandatory 5-year lock-in and the interest earned is still taxable.
