Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

1. Who we are

Vridhi ("we", "us", "our") operates the website vridhi.ink. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and what choices you have.

2. What we collect

  • Contact details you provide: name, email address, and phone number, when you submit the advisor callback form, newsletter signup, or IPO alert signup.
  • Date of birth (and optionally birth time/place), only if you use the Money Horoscope feature — this is used solely to generate that feature's content and is not used for any financial assessment.
  • Usage data: pages visited, device type, approximate location (city/country level), and interactions on the site, collected automatically via Google Analytics (GA4) and Microsoft Clarity.
  • Cookies: used by our analytics providers and, if enabled, by Google AdSense for showing relevant ads.

3. How we use your data

  • To respond to callback requests by passing your details to a partner advisor
  • To send the newsletter or IPO alerts you signed up for
  • To understand site usage and improve the product (via GA4 and Clarity)
  • To show relevant advertising, if and once AdSense is active on this site

4. Third parties we share data with

We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies: Formspree (form submissions), Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity (usage analytics), Google AdSense (advertising, if enabled), Razorpay (payments, if enabled), and partner advisors/lenders/brokers you choose to be connected with via our forms or affiliate links.

5. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to access, correct, and request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@vridhi.ink.

6. Data retention

We retain contact information only as long as needed to provide the service you requested, or until you ask us to delete it.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: support@vridhi.ink
